## Service Description

ToxZen is a moderation tool that:
- Accepts ban appeal submissions from users
- Uses AI to analyze appeal content for toxicity
- Presents shielded summaries to moderators
- Records verdict decisions

## Limitations

- ToxZen's AI analysis is advisory only — all decisions are made by human moderators
- AI accuracy varies; always use your judgment
- The service depends on Reddit's Devvit platform and Google's Gemini API availability
